What it researches

Product information

ProductIQ keeps uploaded identifiers and inventory data, attempts Amazon listing extraction, discovers public competitor listings, calculates pricing scenarios, categorizes the catalog, and builds cross-sell and upsell relationships from inventory you actually carry.

When a source fails

Do not throw away the product

If Amazon or a public search source does not cooperate, ProductIQ keeps the inventory record and shows what could and could not be verified instead of inventing data.
