What it extracts

Listing information

ProductIQ attempts to retrieve title, brand, price, availability, rating, reviews, seller, bullets, description, categories, technical details, dimensions, weight, manufacturer, and product images.

Important limitation

Real pages, real failures

Amazon can change its page structure or show CAPTCHAs. ProductIQ reports those failures instead of creating fake data. Smaller batches and slower retries are more dependable.
